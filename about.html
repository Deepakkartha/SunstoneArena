About Sunstone Sports Arena

A cricket-focused, multi-purpose venue on 65 acres of Arizona desert — designed to international standards and built for players, clubs and families.

Our vision

A home for cricket in the Southwest

Sunstone Sports Arena brings first-class cricket facilities to Arizona. Ten full-size grounds sit on 50 acres, with a further 15 acres given over to the clubhouse, covered practice nets, parking and a presentation plaza.

Every ground follows one standard template — the same boundary, the same three-pitch square, the same sight screens and dugouts — so a club match on Ground 9 plays like a final on Ground 1.

The arena is designed for the desert: heat-tolerant Bermuda outfields, shaded seating and misters, early-morning irrigation and a storm basin that captures monsoon rain from every surface on site.

The master plan

Sunstone at a glance

65 acres

760 × 414 yd site: 50 acres of grounds plus a 15-acre facilities strip along the south.

10 grounds

Two rows of five, 65 yd straight × 70 yd square, with 15-yard gaps and concrete cart paths.

30 pitches

Two natural clay pitches and one astro turf pitch on every ground.

Full amenities

Clubhouse, 6 covered nets, 20 dugouts with bleachers, 250-car parking and a stage plaza.

How we're building it

Development phases

  1. Site preparation
    Grading, caliche ripping, drainage and irrigation mains, storm basin and access road.
  2. Phase 1 — Grounds 1–5
    First five grounds, practice nets, parking and a phase-1 clubhouse.
  3. Phase 2 — Grounds 6–10
    Second row of grounds built by the same crews for continuous, cost-efficient work.
  4. Full clubhouse & plaza
    Dining, viewing terrace, media room, conference rooms and the presentation plaza.

While the main squares are under construction, rolled practice pitches keep cricket going on site.
