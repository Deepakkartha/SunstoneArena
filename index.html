Welcome to Sunstone

Built for the game. Shaped by the desert.

Ten north–south grounds with 65-yard straight and 70-yard square boundaries, each with two natural clay pitches and an astro strip, sight screens at both ends and shaded dugouts for both teams.

  • Heat-loving hybrid Bermuda outfields, overseeded for green winter cricket
  • Australian-style clay pitches for true, fast bounce
  • Covered, lit practice nets — train all day, all season
  • Golf-cart paths, 250-car parking and a presentation plaza
